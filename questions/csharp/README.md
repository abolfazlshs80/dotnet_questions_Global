# C# و مبانی .NET

این بخش مفاهیم زبان C#، runtime، حافظه، هم‌زمانی و LINQ را پوشش می‌دهد.

## انواع، کلاس‌ها و OOP

1. What is the difference between value types and reference types?
2. What is the difference between a `class`, `struct`, and `record`?
3. What are the four principles of object-oriented programming?
4. What is the difference between an abstract class and an interface?
5. Can a C# class inherit from multiple classes? How can it implement multiple interfaces?
6. What are access modifiers, including `protected internal` and `private protected`?
7. What is the difference between method overloading, overriding, and method hiding?
8. What are `virtual`, `abstract`, `sealed`, and `new` used for?
9. What is the difference between `const` and `readonly`?
10. What are constructors, static constructors, and constructor chaining?
11. What are inheritance and composition, and when would you choose one over the other?
12. What are boxing and unboxing?

## زبان و runtime

13. What are delegates and events, and when would you use each?
14. What is the difference between `ref`, `out`, and `in` parameters?
15. What are generics, and what problems do they solve?
16. What are extension methods and partial classes?
17. What is reflection, and what are appropriate use cases for it?
18. What is the difference between `string` and `StringBuilder`?
19. What are the CLR, CTS, and CLS?
20. What is the difference between managed and unmanaged code?
21. How does garbage collection work, and what are its generations?
22. What is the difference between `Dispose` and a finalizer?
23. What are the stack and heap, and how do they relate to value and reference types?

## Async، thread و LINQ

24. What is the difference between a process and a thread?
25. What is the difference between asynchronous programming and multithreading?
26. What are `Task` and `async`/`await`, and when should you use them?
27. How do you run multiple asynchronous operations concurrently?
28. What can cause a deadlock in asynchronous C# code, and how would you diagnose it?
29. What are `IEnumerable<T>` and `IQueryable<T>`, and how do they differ?
30. What is deferred execution in LINQ?
31. How do `FirstOrDefault()` and `SingleOrDefault()` differ?
32. What are expression trees, and how are they used by LINQ providers?
33. What are `yield return` and iterator methods?
34. How do you make shared data safe in multithreaded code?
